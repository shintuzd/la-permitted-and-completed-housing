Methodology

Sources
Los Angeles Department of Building and Safety (LADBS) building permits on the City's open data portal: issued before 2010, 2010–2019, and 2020 to present, plus LADBS Certificates of Occupancy. Neighborhoods are the LA Times neighborhood boundaries. Interest rates are the effective federal funds rate (FEDFUNDS) and Freddie Mac's 30-year fixed mortgage rate (MORTGAGE30US) from FRED, averaged by month.
Units
Units are the sum of dwelling units (du_changed), accessory dwelling units (adu_changed), and junior ADUs (junior_adu) on each permit. "New units" counts only permits that add units; "Net of demolitions" also subtracts units removed by demolitions and conversions.
Permitted
Counted by permit issue date, starting with permits issued January 1, 2001.
Completed
Counted by Certificate of Occupancy (CofO) date. LADBS did not record CofO dates in this data before 2005, so completions start in 2005. When a permit's CofO date is blank but the CofO dataset has one, the earliest CofO issued for that permit is used. Permits with no CofO date are not counted as completed. Some projects may have their TCO, but are considered incomplete in this dashboard given the lack of CofO.
Housing types
ADUs include junior ADUs. Other units are grouped by the number of units on the permit: single-family (1), duplex to fourplex (2–4), and apartments (5+), regardless of the permit's building category, since many mixed-use apartment buildings are permitted as "Commercial." A permit that adds both a home and an ADU is split between the two types.
Pipeline status
Completed permits have a CofO date. In progress: Issued, CofO in Progress, Re-Activate Permit, CofO Reactivated, Not Ready for Inspection, or Permit Extended, issued within the last 5 years. Stalled or abandoned: Permit Expired, Permit Closed, Refund in Progress, No Progress, Intent to Revoke, or an in-progress status more than 5 years after issue. Finaled without a CofO date: Permit Finaled, CofO Corrected, or CofO Superseded with no CofO date on record.
Duplicates
Supplemental permits that repeat the original permit's unit count are excluded, as are non-building permits (such as parking structures) and Addition or Alter/Repair permits that repeat a new building's unit count at the same address. Supplements that change the unit count are kept as revisions.
Locations
Permits without coordinates are located by parcel number, then by address using LA County parcel records and the U.S. Census geocoder, and finally at the center of the Assessor block for parcels that have since been renumbered. Permits that can't be located are still counted in the citywide totals but don't appear on the map or in neighborhood totals.
Corrections
Known data errors are corrected by hand, and projects missing from the LADBS data are added manually. The data refreshes every week.
